04 // OPERATING THESIS

How I Make Decisions

01 // DEMAND > INFRASTRUCTURE

Sell the Receipt Before Pouring the Concrete

Software is cheap; real customer urgency is rare. Having taken a legal-tech platform from blank screen to live users, I never build infrastructure for hypothetical demand. Prove someone will cross the street for the solution manually before automating a single workflow.

02 // ASYMMETRIC TRUST

Design for the Skeptic, Not the Early Adopter

Anyone can delight a tech enthusiast; real businesses are won when a cautious buyer trusts you with something consequential. When contracts, money, or reputation are at stake, clarity beats cleverness—every pixel should reduce perceived risk.

03 // INTELLECTUAL HONESTY

Treat Live Ventures as Experiments, Not Monuments

Ego keeps founders trapped in mediocre businesses. Sunsetting lawsuite.space taught me that the highest-leverage move in venture building is reading market reality without flinching—extracting the structural insight, closing the loop cleanly, and redeploying faster on the next bet.
